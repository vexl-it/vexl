#### **PU₿ QUIZ and ₿TALKS Are Here!**

**10.2.2025** – Vexl is thrilled to launch **PU₿ QUIZ** and **₿TALKS**, the ultimate meetup cards for Bitcoiners. Designed to bring energy, inspiration, and connection to your local Bitcoin meetups, these cards are here to help you learn, laugh, and dive into meaningful discussions about Bitcoin.

This is a **beta launch**, and in the first round, **21 meetups** around the world will be testing the cards. We’re starting small to gather feedback from participants and organizers, ensuring the cards are as engaging and effective as possible.

##### **What Are PU₿ QUIZ and ₿TALKS?**

- **PU₿ QUIZ**: Packed with Bitcoin trivia, these cards are perfect for testing your knowledge, sparking lively debates, and having a lot of fun along the way.
- **₿TALKS**: Dive deeper with thought-provoking conversation starters that explore Bitcoin adoption, privacy, common BTC criticisms, and building strong, local peer-to-peer economies.

**These cards are designed to make meetups more engaging, inclusive, and educational for people new to bitcoin and the old-timers alike.  
**

##### **A Community-Driven Project**

This project wouldn’t have been possible without the incredible support of **community volunteers** who helped translate the cards into **Spanish, German, Italian, and English**. Their contributions have made **PU₿ QUIZ** and **₿TALKS** accessible to Bitcoiners around the world.

At Vexl, community is at the heart of everything we do. Vexl wouldn’t exist without the passion and collaboration of bitcoiners everywhere. If you’d like to be part of this project, we’d love your help translating and localizing the app or the cards into even more languages, so we can reach every Bitcoin community across the globe.

Once the beta phase is complete, we plan to **open-source the cards** and provide them **free to meetups worldwide**. Our goal is to empower Bitcoin communities everywhere with tools that spark connection, learning, and growth.

##### **Golden Glasses: Show You’re Part of the Community**

When you attend a local Bitcoin meetup and participate in **PU₿ QUIZ** and **₿TALKS**, you’ll earn **Golden Glasses**—a badge for your Vexl avatar. These glasses are visible to others with Golden Glasses, showing that you’ve been part of the fun and are actively building your Bitcoin community.

It’s a cool way to connect with others who share your curiosity and enthusiasm for Bitcoin.

Just tap the peer-to-peer card with your NFC and the Vexl app to get your glasses!

##### **Get Out and Get Involved**

If you’re part of one of the **21 beta meetups**, we can’t wait to hear your feedback. And if you’re not, stay tuned—we’ll be opening up **PU₿ QUIZ** and **₿TALKS** to more communities soon.

To learn more about **PU₿ QUIZ**, **₿TALKS**, and how to get started and get a set for your meetup, reach out to us on our social media or via our website [Vexl.it](http://Vexl.it).

##### **About Vexl**

Vexl is a nonprofit, bitcoin-only app that helps you buy and sell bitcoin peer-to-peer, privately, and without KYC.
