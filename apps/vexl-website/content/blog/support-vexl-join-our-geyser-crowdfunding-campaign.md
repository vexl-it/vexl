Hello fellow Vexslaks,

We’ve launched our crowdfunding page on Geyser, and we need your help. Vexl is all about making bitcoin simple: peer-to-peer, without KYC, paperwork, or middlemen. If you want bitcoin, you buy bitcoin—that’s how it should be.

We’re building a nonprofit, open-source app that helps people trade bitcoin directly and foster local economies. But to keep improving the platform and making it available to more people, we need your support.

![](/blog/support-vexl-join-our-geyser-crowdfunding-campaign/66fa6eb9cd2d8ca31589cd5e_vexl-20xmas.webp)

##### **Why Your Support Matters**

By contributing to our Geyser campaign, you’ll help us:

• **Keep improving the Vexl app** with new features based on your feedback.

• **Strengthen local economies** by making it easier for communities to trade bitcoin.

• **Grow the Vexl Foundation**, which supports open-source, privacy-preserving projects like Vexl that prioritize financial freedom and privacy.

We’re not funded by investors—this is a community-driven project. And through Geyser, you can directly support what we’re building.

##### Grab some Merch!

As a thank you for your support, we’re offering rewards designed to help you spread the word about Vexl in your local community. You can grab a Vexl T-shirt, a sticker pack, and more gear that will help you promote Vexl wherever you go. It’s not just about backing a project—it’s about helping grow a movement for peer-to-peer bitcoin.

![](/blog/support-vexl-join-our-geyser-crowdfunding-campaign/66f40b78bcb079ade33cf3f1_merch-20vexl-min.webp)

##### **How to Support Us?**

Visit our Geyser page [**Support Vexl on Geyser**](https://geyser.fund/project/vexl) to contribute. Every bit helps us get closer to a future where bitcoin stays peer-to-peer, privacy-focused, and accessible for all.

Thanks for being part of the Vexl community. Together, we can make a real impact.
