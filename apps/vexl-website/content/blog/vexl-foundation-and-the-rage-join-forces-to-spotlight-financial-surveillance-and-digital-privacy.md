#### **Vexl Foundation and The Rage Join Forces to Spotlight Financial Surveillance and Digital Privacy**

**Prague, Czech Republic** – The Vexl Foundation is proud to announce a new collaboration with _L0la L33tz_ _of The Rage_, a leading independent publication covering financial surveillance, civil liberties, and digital privacy. This partnership reflects the Foundation’s unwavering commitment to defending financial freedom and supporting voices that bring critical issues into the public spotlight.

As part of this collaboration, _The Rage_ will publish a **special six-part editorial series** exploring the most pressing threats to financial privacy and individual autonomy in the digital age. The series will delve into topics such as the global expansion of Know Your Customer (KYC) regulations, the implications of Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs), and the societal consequences of increasingly intrusive financial oversight.

**The first article of the series is now available** to read on [therage.co](https://www.therage.co), inside the Vexl app (iOS & Android), and on the [Vexl's blog](https://vexl.it/blog).

> "Independent journalism is a vital pillar in the fight for financial sovereignty. _The Rage_ consistently brings attention to the risks posed by overreaching regulation and unchecked surveillance,” said **Lea Petrášová**, President of the Vexl Foundation. “We believe in amplifying voices that challenge the status quo and empower individuals with information that matters.”

> "Vexl has created a service that allows anyone to use Bitcoin the way it was intended: by trading directly peer-to-peer. Building a product that is easy to use, it solves a massive problem in the industry where centralized exchanges conventionally hold the upper hand. As such, it is a product that deeply resonates with The Rage’s vision that access to financial services should be available to anyone, anywhere, anytime, without discrimination, regardless of race, religion, income or nationality. I could not be more proud to cooperate with Vexl and play a small part in bringing their vision to life,” said L0la L33tz of The Rage.

This partnership underscores both organizations shared mission: to protect the right to transact freely, to question authority, and to educate the public about the tools and policies shaping the future of money.
