When you create or update an offer, Vexl encrypts it for the people in your network who can see it. We’ve improved how this works, making offer encryption significantly faster.

As the Vexl network grows, faster encryption helps the app stay fast without changing how your offers are privately shared across your network.

You may notice that your potential reach in your profile is now lower. We’ve made this number more accurate by excluding less active users from your reach.
