# Vexl Brand Narrative & Tone of Voice

This folder is the source of truth for **how Vexl talks**. Use it to:

1. **Audit existing copy** — does this string sound like Vexl? Is it human? Are the terms right?
2. **Write new copy** — app strings, push notifications, marketing, social, blog, support replies, legal.

It's written so a human writer _or_ an AI agent can pick it up and produce or review copy that sounds consistently like Vexl.

---

## The one thing to remember

> **Sound human. Not like a bank.**

Everything else in this guide is a footnote to that. Vexl exists because the financial world is cold, gated, and surveils you. Our copy has to feel like the opposite: a real person, on your side, who happens to be a bit of a rebel about money and privacy.

If a sentence could appear verbatim on a banking app or an exchange's compliance page, rewrite it.

## Who Vexl is (the 30-second version)

Vexl is a mobile app to **buy and sell Bitcoin peer-to-peer, without KYC**, through your real-world social network — friends and friends of friends. No middlemen, no surveillance, no ID checks. Vexl can't see your contacts, your chats, or your trades. You stay anonymous until _you_ decide to reveal yourself.

- **What we sell:** freedom to transact, privacy as a practical option, and trust between real people.
- **Who we talk to:** normies and newcomers first — but OGs and hardcore bitcoiners should still feel at home.
- **Who's behind it:** the non-profit Vexl Foundation. Open source. Not for profit.

---

## Audience priority

Write for **normies and newcomers first.** They decide whether Vexl grows.

- Don't assume the reader knows what KYC, on-chain, sats, or a private key are. Explain in plain language or avoid jargon.
- But never dumb it down to the point that an experienced bitcoiner rolls their eyes. Respect the reader.
- The test: a curious friend who just heard "you can buy Bitcoin without giving your ID" should understand every sentence.
