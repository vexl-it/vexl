# Terminology & Glossary

The fastest way to sound like Vexl (or break it) is word choice. This file is the rulebook. When auditing, terminology violations are the easiest things to catch — start here.

---

## Forbidden terms (do not use)

| ❌ Forbidden                                                                                  | ✅ Use instead                                                                                                             | Why                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Vexlak / Vexlaks / Vexler / vexlers**                                                       | **Vexl user**, **user**, **people**, **the other person**, **friends and friends of friends**, **someone in your network** | Insider jargon confuses newcomers. We write for normies first. Only fall back to "Vexlak" if there is genuinely no other way to express the intent — and that should be rare. |
| **counterparty**                                                                              | **the other person**                                                                                                       | "Counterparty" sounds like a bank or a brokerage. We are deliberately _not_ that. Sound human.                                                                                |
| **other side / the other side**                                                               | **the other person**                                                                                                       | Same reason — warmer and clearer.                                                                                                                                             |
| **KYC'd / un-KYC'd** (as casual verbs in user copy)                                           | "without KYC", "no ID required", "no identity checks"                                                                      | Keep it plain for newcomers. (Explaining "KYC" in marketing is fine; see below.)                                                                                              |
| Corporate/process verbs: **dispatch, authenticate, provision, facilitate, utilize, leverage** | text/send, confirm, set up, help, use                                                                                      | These are institutional tells.                                                                                                                                                |
| Hype: **Don't miss out!**, **Act now!**, **Limited time!!!**                                  | calm, confident invitations                                                                                                | We're a movement, not a funnel.                                                                                                                                               |

> **Migration note:** the current app still contains many instances of "vexlak(s)", "counterparty", and "other side". Treat every one of these as an **audit finding to fix**, not as precedent to copy.

## The user

There is **no special name for a Vexl user.** That's intentional — naming the tribe ("Vexlak") creates an in-group that newcomers don't belong to yet.

Refer to people as, depending on context:

- **you / your friends / people you know**
- **the other person** (the human on the other end of a trade or chat)
- **people in your network**, **friends and friends of friends**
- **a Vexl user / users** (neutral, when you must be generic)

## Bitcoin: capitalization & terms

Follow the standard bitcoiner convention:

- **"Bitcoin"** (capital B) = the **network, the protocol, the idea, the movement**.
  - _"Bitcoin as it was intended."_ · _"Take back your freedom to transact with Bitcoin."_
- **"bitcoin"** (lowercase b) = **the money / the asset / an amount** you buy, sell, send, or hold.
  - _"Buy bitcoin without KYC."_ · _"Send some bitcoin as a donation."_
- **"BTC"** = the **ticker/unit**, best next to numbers. _"0.05 BTC"_, _"1 BTC = …"_.
- **"sats"** (lowercase) = satoshis, the small unit. Fine for amounts; expand to "satoshis (the smallest unit of bitcoin)" the first time in newcomer-facing marketing.

Quick test: if you could swap in "the dollar" → capital "Bitcoin"; if you could swap in "some dollars" → lowercase "bitcoin".

> The app currently mixes "Buy Bitcoin" / "buy bitcoin" / "Sell Bitcoin". Under this rule the asset is lowercase: **"Buy bitcoin" / "Sell bitcoin"**. Flag inconsistencies in audits.

## Core vocabulary (use these)

These phrases already carry the brand. Reuse them; don't reinvent.

- **"friends and friends of friends"** — our signature description of the network. Also: "your real-world social network", "people you know and trust".
- **"peer-to-peer" / "P2P"** — how trades happen. (Spell out "peer-to-peer" first for newcomers; "P2P" after.)
- **"no KYC" / "without KYC" / "no ID required"** — our key differentiator.
- **"end-to-end encrypted"** — for chats/messages.
- **"reveal your identity"** — the act of choosing to show who you are. Always framed as the user's choice, reversible-awareness included ("once you reveal it, it can't be taken back").
- **"reach"** — how many people can see your offer ("You reach 1,200 people").
- **"offer"** — what users post (to buy or sell). Not "listing" or "ad" in user copy.
- **"trade" / "deal"** — the transaction between two people.
- **"your network"** — the people you can reach.
- **"Vexl Clubs"** — communities that extend reach beyond direct contacts.
- **"the Vexl Foundation"** — the non-profit behind Vexl.

## Concepts to explain, not assume (for newcomers)

When these first appear in newcomer-facing copy, give a half-sentence of plain context rather than assuming knowledge:

| Term             | Plain framing                                                    |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| KYC              | "identity checks (KYC) that exchanges force on you"              |
| On-chain         | "On-chain — best for larger amounts, slower but more secure"     |
| Lightning        | "Lightning — fast and cheap, best for smaller amounts"           |
| Private key      | "the key that lives only on your phone and unlocks your account" |
| sats / satoshis  | "sats — the smallest unit of bitcoin"                            |
| 1st / 2nd degree | "friends (1st degree) and friends of friends (2nd degree)"       |

## Naming & casing quick reference

- **Vexl** — always capital V, never "vexl" or "VEXL" in prose.
- **Vexl Clubs**, **Vexl Foundation**, **Vexl user** — capital V, descriptor capitalized when it's a proper name (Clubs, Foundation).
- **Marketplace, Chats, Community, Map** — capitalize when referring to the named app tabs/sections; lowercase as common nouns.
- Avoid ALL CAPS for emphasis. Use it only for genuine acronyms (BTC, KYC, P2P, QR).
