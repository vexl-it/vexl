# Voice & Personality

> Read this first. It's the _why_ behind every rule in the other files.

## Brand archetype

Vexl sits on a gradient between the **Explorer** and the **Rebel**, leaning **Explorer**.

- **Explorer** — we empower independence and self-reliance. We show people a way out and let them walk it themselves. We don't preach; we open the door.
- **Rebel** — the whole concept goes against the conventional, KYC'd, surveilled financial system. We're cheeky and unafraid to call out gatekeepers.
- **Innovator** — there's nothing quite like Vexl. We're fresh and open-minded, not weighed down by how things "have always been done."

In one line: **an Explorer with a sharper tongue and a brave spirit.**

> _"Cheeky but trustworthy — it's still about money."_

That tension is the heart of the voice. We can be playful, witty, even a little provocative — but the moment we're talking about someone's money or safety, trust wins. Never sacrifice clarity or honesty for a joke.

## The five voice pillars

### 1. Human, not institutional (the north star)

We sound like a real person who's on your side, not a service, a bank, or a compliance department. We use "you" and "we". We don't hide behind passive, lawyer-y, or corporate phrasing. When something is annoying or scary (logging in, deleting an account, a failed payment), we acknowledge it like a person would.

- ✅ "We'll text you a code to confirm it's really you."
- ❌ "A verification code will be dispatched to the provided number for authentication purposes."

### 2. Privacy is optionality, not ideology

We never frame privacy as paranoia or extremism. It's a practical, sensible choice — _you_ decide what to share and when. Control language ("unless you choose to", "until you decide") does the heavy lifting.

- ✅ "Your number stays private unless you choose to share it."
- ❌ "Protect yourself from surveillance and stay off the grid."

### 3. Empowering, never gatekeeping

We hand people control and get out of the way. We don't approve, verify, vet, or permit users. There are no gatekeepers in our language — because there are none in the product.

- ✅ "You're in. Let's find your friends on Vexl."
- ❌ "Your account has been approved. You may now proceed."

### 4. Clear above clever

Cheeky is great, but never at the cost of being understood — especially by a newcomer. If a clever line risks confusing someone about their money, safety, or what a button does, cut it. Clarity is part of trust.

### 5. Confident and warm, not hype-y

We're enthusiastic and proud, but we don't oversell, beg, or use growth-hack desperation ("Don't miss out!!!", "Act now!"). We're a movement, not a marketing funnel.

## How the voice flexes by context

The personality is constant; the dial moves:

- **Onboarding & empty states** — most warmth and encouragement. Welcome people, reduce friction, celebrate small wins ("You're in!", "You're early").
- **Marketplace / trade flow** — clear and confident, lightly upbeat. Get out of the way.
- **Safety & money moments** (sending funds, revealing identity, deleting data) — calm, direct, plain. Drop the jokes. Be the trustworthy friend who tells it straight: "Always money before BTC."
- **Errors** — human and helpful. Say what happened, what to do next, and don't blame the user.
- **Marketing & social** — most room to be bold, witty, and provocative. Lead with the narratives (see [`narrative-playbook.md`](./narrative-playbook.md)).
- **Legal (Terms/Privacy)** — still unmistakably Vexl. Our Terms are warm and self-aware ("law dictates that we must be more precise, so let's get it over with"). Even here, we don't disappear behind legalese.

## Litmus tests

Before shipping a line, ask:

1. **Would a real person say this to a friend?** If not, rewrite.
2. **Could this exact sentence live on a bank or exchange app?** If yes, it's too institutional.
3. **Does it hand the reader control, or take it away?**
4. **Would a complete newcomer understand it?**
5. **If it's a joke — is the meaning still 100% clear without it?**
